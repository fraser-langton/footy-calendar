# Footy Calendar

Operate mode. One page, readable on a phone. Warm near-white, dark grass green, hairline dividers, compact native checkbox controls. Small football mark and a quiet pitch drawing tie it to footy; the form owns the screen. Competition and club selection stay visible; secondary filters sit in a disclosure. The subscription panel immediately follows filters on mobile and sits alongside on desktop. All controls have visible keyboard focus. Plain, direct copy; no marketing sections.
